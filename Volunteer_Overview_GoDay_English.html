Go Day 2026

Saturday, May 16, 2026
Kupini, La Paz, Bolivia
Go Day is a Crossroads Church tradition where the whole community steps out of the building and into people's lives — showing up for real families with real needs. One day, all hands on deck, together.

This year we're serving three families in the Kupini community. It's a full day of hands-on work, and there's a role for every person who shows up. You don't need construction experience. You just need to come. This is what giving looks like — coming together, rolling up our sleeves, and creating real change in the lives of the people around us.
How you can help
🧱 Carry & move materials
🪣 Mix & pass cement
🔧 Install pipes & drainage
🛏️ Assemble furniture
🥪 Make & hand out sandwiches
💧 Keep everyone hydrated
👶 Watch over the kids
📸 Capture the day
🙏 Pray & encourage
The three projects
Project 1
Rebuilding Celia's Wall

Celia is a mother of four and one of Veysú's most devoted community volunteers. Last rainy season, a landslide brought down the exterior wall of her home on the hillside. Together we're going to rebuild it.

What we'll do
  • Carry and organize rebar, cement, and sand
  • Mix cement and pass bricks down the line
  • Keep the work site running smoothly
  • Feed and hydrate the team throughout the day
Project 2
Drainage for Patricia's Home

Patricia's home has flooded more times than she can count. Living on the hillside means every rainy season sends water straight to her foundation. We'll install PVC drainage channels to finally redirect that water and protect her home.

What we'll do
  • Dig and prepare the drainage path
  • Cut and connect PVC drainage pipes
  • Direct the team and handle materials
  • Keep the crew fed and hydrated
Project 3
A Bunk Bed for David's Girls

David is a widower and blacksmith raising seven children in a small home divided by hanging fabric curtains. His three daughters share a single bed. We're purchasing and assembling a two-level metal bunk bed with full bedding — David, being a blacksmith, will lead the assembly. His son Davidson and daughter Marioly were baptized on May 1st.

What we'll do
  • Receive and carry the bunk bed and bedding
  • Assist David with assembly
  • Make the beds and set everything up
  • Help reorganize the home's living spaces so the family can make better use of what they have
  • Celebrate with the family
